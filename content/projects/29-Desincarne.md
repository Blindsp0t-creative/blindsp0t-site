  - 'DESINCARNÉ is a hybrid performance between circus, video and programming, bringing together Elza Davidson (contortionist), Alex Trillaud (Chinese mast acrobat), Fanny Montlouis (graphic designer/vjay), Antoine Vanel and Benjamin Petit (programmers of the Beam''Art Collective), Gael Multier, with music by Pierre Herman.<br>

    <br>Suitable for digital arts or circus festivals, this 20-minute show creates an exchange with the public to question its own relationship to the body and the virtual.'
