    <br>

    La fin du présent (The End of the Present) is an immersive, hybrid, and multifaceted show based on three plays by Maurice Maeterlinck: Intérieur (Interior), L’intruse (The Intruder), and Les sept princesses (The Seven Princesses). These profound texts revolve around our individual and collective relationship with death. Together, they give rise to a three-part show, permeated by the notion of collapse, whether of nature, a family, a kingdom, or youth; a strange mirror of the crisis of life we are currently experiencing.<br>

    <br>

    It all begins in the garden of a house, where we are unsure whether to enter; then we move to a living room with a family helpless in the face of the strange reactions of the blind patriarch; and finally we end up in the heart of an imaginary kingdom, symbolizing lost youth. A journey to the heart of our existence, each part of which questions our relationship with the world.<br>

    <br>

    We imagine a total spectacle taking place throughout the entire theater, where spectators will be immersed in the work and will change position and therefore perception. In La fin du présent, it is a matter of traversing the ruins of our existence, as if we were ghosts of ourselves. These three texts reveal the absurdity of our condition, like a distorting mirror. La fin du présent is also about the coexistence of realities which, when they collide, cause such an explosion that history ends, without explanation. We imagine a disturbing stage production, placing the audience on the threshold of another world, a space-time in which we are all dying.<br><br>Production: INVIVO // Co-production: Les Gémeaux, national theater of Sceaux / OARA, Nouvelle-Aquitaine Regional Arts Office / Théâtre de l’Union, Limousin National Theater / Théâtre Nouvelle Génération, Lyon National Theater / Théâtre Ducourneau - Agen / Prix Spectacle Vivant - Scène Numérique 2023 / Le lieu unique, scène nationale de Nantes / Stereolux - Nantes / L’Hexagone, scène nationale de Meylan // Support: CNC - Fonds d’aide à la création immersive / DRAC Nouvelle-Aquitaine / Région Nouvelle-Aquitaine, programme Cultures Connectées / GEIQ compagnonnage théâtre / Grenier à Sel - Avignon / Villa Creative / Espace d’Albret – Nérac / INVIVO is accredited by DRAC Nouvelle-Aquitaine, supported by the Nouvelle Aquitaine Region and the City of Agen, and accredited by the Department of Lot-et-Garonne // For the development of the project: INVIVO has been approved by DRAC Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes 22-23 / SCAN Fund – DRAC and Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes Region.'
